YT Creator’s Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. We encourage users to review the respective Privacy Policies of third-party ad servers or websites for more detailed information about their practices and instructions on how to opt out of certain options.

Children’s Information

Protecting children’s privacy is especially important to us. YT Creator does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.

If you believe that your child has provided personal information on our website, please contact us immediately and we will take appropriate action to remove such information from our records.
